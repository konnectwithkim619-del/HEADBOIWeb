About Me

Hello, my name is Kim. I earned my Bachelor of Science degree from the University of San Francisco before graduating from the University of Illinois College of Medicine in 1994.

I met the love of my life, Trevor, on an airplane in 1990 — a serendipitous encounter that would become the heartbeat of my story. We married twice: once in 1990, swept up in young love, and again in July 1991 among family and friends, sealing a promise that would follow us across decades, storms, and second chances.

After completing my pediatric residency in Phoenix, Trevor and I built a life in Arizona, raising our three children — Austin, Kyle, and Keelyn — with love, grit, and faith. I devoted my career to caring for children, beginning in private practice and later as a hospitalist, before discovering my true calling in the pediatric emergency room. There, I comforted families during their most vulnerable moments, offering steady hands and a steady heart.

A rare medical condition ended my ER career unexpectedly, forcing me to redefine who I was outside of medicine. In that difficult shift, I rediscovered creativity, resilience, and joy — through cake decorating, drawing, and learning piano — each becoming part of my healing and a new way to celebrate life.

My own journey through addiction, loss, and recovery gave me a renewed purpose: to shine a compassionate light on gambling addiction, especially among women, and to remind others that redemption is possible even after the fiercest storms. I now live in Arizona, where I still find solace and clarity on the ice — a sanctuary I have loved since childhood — and in the laughter of my granddaughter, Charlie, and my grandson, Ethan, who complete my family’s own double rainbow.
