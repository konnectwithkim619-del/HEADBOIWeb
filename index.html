Welcome to KonnectWithKim

Dr. Kim Steffes spent her entire life defying the odds. Growing up in a chaotic household surrounded by turmoil, she became the only one of her siblings to finish college, ultimately climbing to the pinnacle of medical success as a pediatric emergency room physician. For decades, she was the anchor—the one who took care of everyone: her patients, her husband, her three children, and her extended family. But when a sudden disability stripped away her identity and prescription medication triggered severe obsessive-compulsive behaviors, Kim found a dangerous new escape. In a whirlwind storm of compulsion, she gambled away everything: her life savings, her medical dignity, and her marriage. Structured as a living soundtrack—where every chapter is a song that scored her highest highs and lowest rock bottoms—this raw, vulnerable memoir exposes the hidden crisis of high-achieving medical professionals facing burnout and addiction. Moving from the heavy darkness of the tempest to the radiant light of a 12-step recovery, Happily Ever After? Don’t Bet on It. is not just a story of ruin; it is a roadmap to resurrection. It proves that even after the most catastrophic wreckage, a double rainbow is possible, offering a deeply moving testament to gratitude, accountability, and a marriage rebuilt stronger than ever.
